# Play Store listing text

## App name (30 characters max)
Saathi: Azan & Prayer Times

## Short description (80 characters max)
Accurate azan, prayer times, qibla, tracker and reminders. Works offline.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

Saathi is a calm, accurate prayer companion that works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.

AZAN THAT REALLY SOUNDS
- Prayer times for Fajr, Dhuhr (Jumma on Fridays), Asr, Maghrib and Isha, worked out on your phone
- Choose your calculation method and Hanafi or Shafi Asr. The right method is picked for your country
- Azan with a real recording, a short version or a simple tone
- Play the azan on the alarm volume so you hear it even in silent mode
- A "Check my setup" screen tells you if anything stops the azan, with a fix button for each problem
- Optional heads-up before each prayer

NEVER MISS A PRAYER
- "Prayer time is ending" reminders that repeat until you have prayed
- Optional photo proof: take a picture of your prayer place to stop the reminders. The photo is
  checked on your phone and is never saved or sent
- "Stop reminding me" is always one tap away

MOSQUE JAMA'AT
- Enter your mosque's jama'at times, as minutes after the azan or a fixed time, plus Jumma
- A "time to leave" reminder before jama'at
- Optionally silence your phone during jama'at and bring it back afterwards

TRACK YOUR HABIT
- Tick prayers for today or fix past days
- Streaks, a weekly view and a pause for illness or a woman's period, so those days never count as missed
- Qaza tracker for missed prayers, with a starting balance, make-up buttons and history

MORE
- Qibla compass
- Monthly timetable
- Hijri date with adjustment
- Home-screen widget with a live countdown to the next prayer
- English, Urdu and Roman Urdu
- Optional cloud backup of your prayer log, with no account. You can delete it any time

PRIVACY
Your data stays on your phone. Cloud backup is off unless you turn it on, and it only ever uploads
which prayers you ticked. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

Notes: for the azan to sound on time, allow notifications and "Alarms & reminders", and set battery to
"No restrictions". The app shows you exactly how.
